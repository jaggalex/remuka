## When you need it

You need to discuss an object or a point in the room without moving the conversation to a chat. The comment stays next to the place it belongs to.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in and have access to the room.
- Your role is **Commenter**, **Editor**, **Co-owner** or **Owner**.

## Steps

1. Click the room name and choose **More → Comments**. The number beside the room name shows how many comments are unread. To open a particular thread, click its pin on the canvas.
2. Click **New comment**. With one object selected, the pin starts near its top-right corner. With nothing selected, click the spot you want on an object or the canvas.
3. Enter the text and send it.
4. Open a thread to read replies; the **Reply… @ to mention** field answers in it.
5. **Resolve** (the green button) closes the thread and moves it to the resolved tab; a reply opens it again.
6. The author may delete their own comment; a room manager may delete any.

The thread author and a room manager can drag the pin. Drop it on an object to keep it attached when the object moves, rotates or changes size. Drop it on the empty canvas to leave it at that point. Press **Ctrl+Z** or **⌘Z** to undo.

## Check

- Threads, replies and pins are still there after a reload; a pin sits on the object or point.
- Deleting an object does not delete the thread.
- The **Commenter** role writes in the discussion but does not change objects.
- A link visitor does not see the discussion.
- With everything closed: “Every question is closed. New ones appear here — and as a pin on the canvas.”
- Opening comments closes the properties panel: one panel, and `Esc` closes it.

## In presentation and export

- In presentation mode the pins show comments of the current slide; the panel opens as usual.
- When exporting PNG/PDF, enable **Include comments**: a thread list appears below the room and numbered pins on the objects. Resolved threads are not printed.

## Limits

- Comments are online; a reply draft is kept, sending waits for the network and says so.
- One anchor per thread; no nested threads.
- Mentions and notifications are not in v1; the author name is shown with the `@handle` it had when sent.
- PNG/PDF include only open threads and at most three messages per thread; the full text stays in Remuka.
