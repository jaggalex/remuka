# Remuka Data Processing Agreement

Version 1, effective [YYYY-MM-DD]. **Draft for legal review — not published.**

This agreement under Article 28 GDPR is between the customer that holds an organisation at
https://app.remuka.com (“Customer”, controller) and [Full Name], sole proprietor, OGRNIP
[number], [postal address] (“Processor”). It forms part of the Terms of Service.

## 1. Subject and duration

The Processor processes Customer Personal Data in the Customer’s organisation and rooms for as
long as the Customer uses the service, and for the deletion periods in section 9.

## 2. Nature and purpose

Hosting, real-time synchronisation, backup and display of room content to members the
Customer authorises. AI processing happens only when a member starts an agent task.

## 3. Types of data and data subjects

- **Data:** whatever the Customer’s members put into rooms; members’ names and email
  addresses; guests’ display names.
- **Data subjects:** the Customer’s staff, contractors, clients and other people mentioned in
  room content.

## 4. Processor obligations

The Processor:

1. processes data only on the Customer’s documented instructions — these terms and the
   Customer’s use of the product settings;
2. ensures people with access are bound by confidentiality;
3. applies the measures in Annex 2;
4. helps the Customer answer data-subject requests and carry out impact assessments;
5. notifies the Customer of a personal-data breach without undue delay and no later than
   [48] hours after becoming aware of it;
6. makes available the information needed to show compliance.

## 5. Sub-processors

The Customer authorises the sub-processors in Annex 3. The Processor gives 30 days’ notice of
a new sub-processor by email and at https://remuka.com/legal/subprocessors. The Customer may
object; if no solution is found, the Customer may terminate with a refund of the unused
period.

## 6. International transfers

Where data leaves the EEA, the Standard Contractual Clauses (module [2 or 3]) apply.

[Counsel: the Processor is established in Russia. Assess the applicable module, including
module 4 where relevant, and the transfer impact assessment.]

## 7. Audits

Once a year the Processor answers a reasonable security questionnaire and provides
documentation. An on-site audit is possible at the Customer’s cost with 30 days’ notice and
under confidentiality.

## 8. Liability

As in the Terms of Service.

## 9. Return and deletion

When the subscription ends, the Customer can export rooms for 30 days. The Processor then
deletes Customer Personal Data, and it leaves the backups within 14 days.
