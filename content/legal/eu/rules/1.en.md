## 1. What is not allowed

You may not use Remuka to:

1. store or share illegal content;
2. infringe copyright, trademarks or other people’s privacy;
3. upload other people’s personal data without a lawful basis;
4. use public room links for phishing, spam or malware;
5. get around plan limits, create accounts automatically, guess passwords, or load the service
   far beyond normal use;
6. probe for vulnerabilities in ways that disrupt the service or touch other people’s data.
   Report findings through https://github.com/jaggalex/remuka/security or to
   security@remuka.com;
7. resell access to the hosted service without an agreement with us. Running Remuka on your
   own server under the AGPL-3.0 is allowed, following
   https://github.com/jaggalex/remuka/blob/main/TRADEMARKS.md.

2. We review notices within [5] business days.
3. If we act — disable a public link, hide content or suspend access — we tell the organisation

## 3. AI agents

1. Agents use the model provider and key you choose. You pay the provider directly. The agent
   budget in Remuka limits the number of tasks, not the provider’s bill.
2. When you start a task, its text and the content you selected are sent to that provider.
   Do not give an agent anything you may not send to that provider.
3. Agent output is a proposal, drawn with a dashed outline on the canvas. It may be inaccurate
   or wrong. Nothing changes in the room until a person applies it.
4. Every model call is recorded in the room’s log.
5. Provider keys are stored encrypted, shown once when saved, and decrypted only for the
   duration of a task.
