# Remuka Cookie Policy

Version 1, effective [YYYY-MM-DD]. **Draft for legal review — not published.**

Remuka (https://remuka.com, https://app.remuka.com, https://docs.remuka.com) stores a few
cookies and local-storage entries in your browser. They keep you signed in, keep your edits
safe when the connection drops, and remember settings you chose.

**We use no analytics or advertising cookies.** If we ever add them, they will run only after
you agree, and you will be able to withdraw that choice in “Cookie settings”.

Strictly necessary storage is used without consent under Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy
Directive. The European Data Protection Board reads that article as covering local storage
too, so we list both.

## What we store

| Name | Type | Why | Lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `remo_session` | cookie | keeps you signed in | 30 days |
| `remo_csrf` | cookie | protects against forged requests | 30 days |
| `remo_link_unlock` | cookie | access through a password-protected link | 12 hours |
| `remo_visitor` | cookie | one vote per guest; a random number, no name | 1 year |
| `remo_consent` | cookie | your cookie-settings choice | 6 months |
| `remo.identity`, `remo.session` | local storage | your name and colour in a room | until you clear it |
| `remo.board`, `remo.board.backup`, `remo.inspector` | local storage | offline edits and the editor | until edits are sent |
| `remo.lang`, `remo.theme`, `remo.settings` | local storage | language, theme and settings you chose | until you clear it |
| `remo.canvas.hint`, `remo.verifyBanner.*`, `remo.moveBanner.dismissed` | local storage | not showing a hint or notice twice | until you clear it |
| Support chat | cookie inside the support window | your support conversation; loads only when you open the chat | [to confirm] |

## How to remove them

You can delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. You will then need to sign
in again, and offline edits that were not yet sent will be lost.
