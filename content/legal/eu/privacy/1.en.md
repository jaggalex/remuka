## 5. Your rights

You have the right to:
- access your data and get a copy;
- correct it;
- have it erased;
- restrict or object to processing;
- data portability;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
- complain to a supervisory authority.

Write to privacy@remuka.com. We answer within one month.

You can change your name, export your rooms and delete your account yourself in Settings
(Account → Delete account). The account stops working at once; data is erased after 14 days
and leaves the backups within the next 14 days.

## 7. Security

We use:
- TLS for every connection;
- encryption of stored secrets;
- isolation of each organisation’s data in the database;
- role-based access;
- activity logs;
- daily backups kept for 14 days;
- access to production limited to a small number of named people.

We report a personal-data breach that is likely to risk your rights to the supervisory
authority within 72 hours. We inform you without undue delay when the risk is high.

## 9. Automated decisions

only propose changes that a person reviews and applies.
