## Result

The person gets access to the room with the chosen role, and you can see where the role comes
from: directly, through a team or through the organisation. The guest link opens the room
without an account, view only.

## Before you start

- You need the Invite permission in the room. Only an owner or co-owner can change a team role
  and assign a co-owner.
- The person needs an account with the email you enter. If there is no account, an invitation
  link appears; send it yourself.

## Steps

1. Click **Invite** in the room header.
2. Enter the **colleague's email**, choose a role and click **Invite**. Result: the person
   appears under **Already in the room** and a letter with the link goes to their address.
3. Check the source in **Already in the room**: Directly, Through team … or Through
   organisation …. To change a role, choose another one in the row; to revoke access, click
   **Remove**.
4. Turn on the **Guest link**. Result: the address and a **Copy** button appear; a guest opens
   the room without an account and says who they are. Turn the switch off to close the link
   for everyone.
5. Tick **Password** if the link needs protection: enter a password of 8+ characters and click
   **Set password**.
6. Tick **Close after 7 days** if the link should stop working by itself. An exact date can be
   set next to it, in the expiry field.

If Remuka gives you an invitation link, send it to the recipient. They can use the link to sign
in or create an account with the invited email address, then click **Accept invitation**.

## Check the result

- The person can open the room after signing in; their resulting role is shown in **Your role**.
- If a person has several sources, all rows are visible and there is one resulting role: the
  strongest one.
- The guest link opens the room in a private window; with a password it asks for one.

## If it did not work

- **Invite copies the link instead of opening the dialog.** The role cannot share: viewer,
  commenter, guest, or an editor when policy forbids it.
- **A team role does not change.** Only an owner or co-owner can do that.
- **The person cannot open the room.** Check that they signed in with the account you granted
  and that access was not revoked.
- **The link will not turn on.** Unassign the agent first: a public link and an agent cannot
  work in one room.
