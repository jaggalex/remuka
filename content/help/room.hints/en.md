## Result

At the bottom of the room, in the centre, one hint says what can be done here: "Double-click to edit · drag a line's blue dot to reshape it · drag a label to move it". It waits until you have learned the action instead of disappearing on a timer.

The heading in an empty room changes on your next visit. Double-click the canvas to add your first sticker. If you can invite people and have the room to yourself, one of the headings suggests inviting a teammate.

## When it goes away

- after three edits by double-click;
- or after five visits to the room, if the edit was never needed.

Then the hint does not come back. While voting runs, the voting line takes its place and the hint is not shown.

## Bring the hints back

Account menu (the avatar) → **Show the hints again**. Every hint returns, including the ones you have already learned.

The count lives in this browser, not on the account: on another device the hint waits for its first visit again. Reset works only in the browser where you pressed it.
