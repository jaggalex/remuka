## Result

The tools sit in one panel on the left: seven — select, sticky note, shape, text, connector, frame, picture — and **More**. Undo and redo are in the same panel under the hairline, not on a second button.

## How it works

- The chosen tool sits on a yellow marker chip with an ink outline. Clicking the active tool, or the marker in its corner, opens its variants: the colour of a new note, the shape kind, the connector type. The choice is remembered in this browser: the next object is the same.
- **More** opens card, table, kanban, comment and templates — the same five rows `⌘K` finds. A comment asks for a point on the canvas at once.
- Above a selection sits its bar. It has no more than seven buttons and **⋯**: a note has colour, text size, bold, checklist, link, connect and **⋯**; a shape has fill, border, width, kind, lift, text, connect and **⋯**. **⋯** opens the one properties panel on the right.
- Comments, properties and the agent share one panel. Opening comments closes properties; `Esc` closes the panel.
- **Edit text** is in the properties panel and in `⌘K`; a double-click on an object does the same.

## Check

- The rail has seven tools and **More**; undo and redo are there too.
- Clicking an active shape opens five kinds; the chosen kind applies to the next shape.
- A note's bar has seven buttons, a shape's eight (the lift), a connector's seven.
- **⋯** opens the properties panel, not a menu.
- Opening comments closes properties; clicking a pin opens its thread.

## Limits

- The rail is not configurable or reorderable.
- The bar shows the frequent controls; the rest is in **More settings**, the context menu and `⌘K`.
- The order will be revised from usage data (Pulse), but not by the product itself.
