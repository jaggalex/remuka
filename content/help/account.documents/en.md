## Result

You can see which documents this installation publishes, which revision you accepted and when, and withdraw a separate personal-data processing consent where one exists.

## Before you start

- You can open the documents without signing in: the home footer and the account menu both carry a «Documents» entry.
- The acceptance status and the buttons appear only in an account.

## Steps

1. Open the account menu — the circle with your name in the top-right corner — and choose **Documents**.
2. Each document shows the revision in force and its effective date. The title opens the full text in a new tab.
3. If it says “Not accepted”, click **Accept**. For a required document the same action is offered in the window that appears at sign-in.
4. A document with a separate consent has a **Withdraw consent** button. It appears only where the document allows withdrawal.

## Withdrawing consent

The service cannot work without processing your name and e-mail. Withdrawal does not delete anything by itself: it offers account deletion, and the data is erased within the period the document states.

## Check

- On the Documents page an accepted document shows its date; an unaccepted one says “Not accepted”.
- The status survives a reload: the server keeps it, not the browser.
- A guest sees the same documents without a status and without buttons.

## Limits

- A specific revision is accepted: when a document changes, the next sign-in asks you to read and accept the new one.
- Only the consent a document allows can be withdrawn; the offer and the Terms of Service cannot be withdrawn this way.
- Acceptances and withdrawals enter the installation's journal with the revision, the text hash and the time; deleting an account does not erase the fact of acceptance.
