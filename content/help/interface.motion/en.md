## What it is

"Calm animations" is an account setting for people whom motion on screen distracts. A colleague's cursor appears at its new point at once, without a spring or a trailing chase; interface transitions do not drag; objects do not float.

## Turn it on and off

Account menu (the avatar) → **Calm animations**. The check mark shows that it is on; pressing it again turns it off.

## Where it applies

- In this browser — at once.
- In another browser — after signing in to the same account: the setting lives on the server with the theme and language.
- The browser or system "reduce motion" setting works without it; together they mean the same thing — no motion.

## What does not change

Canvas content, colours, fonts and object positions. The setting changes motion only, not the look of the room.
