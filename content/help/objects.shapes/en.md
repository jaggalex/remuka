## Result

A shape of the size you drew is on the board: a rectangle, a rounded rectangle, an ellipse, a diamond or a triangle. You can switch its kind later — text, colour, size and connectors stay where they are.

## Steps

1. Click **Shape** in the rail, press `R` for a rectangle or `O` for an ellipse. The caret beside the tool opens all five kinds with their names and keys.
2. Drag across the board — the shape takes the size you drew. The plate beside it shows width and height. `Shift` makes a square or a circle, `Alt` grows it from the press point.
3. Release — the shape is selected and the rail is back on **Select**. A short click without a drag places the default shape at the centre; a second press of `R` or `O` does the same.
4. Select a shape and open **Kind ▾** on its bar: five silhouettes. **Sharp** and **Rounded** switch the rectangle; on the other kinds the switch is disabled and says why.
5. `Esc` cancels the chosen tool. A double click on **Shape** keeps the tool for a series — also until `Esc`.

## Check

- A shape drawn by dragging has exactly the size the plate showed, at any zoom.
- After a kind switch, text, colour, size and connectors are still there; one undo brings the previous kind back.
- A connector meets the drawn outline: the slanted side of a triangle, the nearest point of a diamond.
- A second participant sees the new shape and the kind switch in real time.

## Limits

- **Sharp** and **Rounded** exist only for a rectangle: they are two of its kinds, not a separate field.
- No new kinds are added; there is no hatching or hand-drawn style.
- In **View only** the tool does not arm: a room editor creates shapes.
