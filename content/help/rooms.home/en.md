## Result

You see the team home: a greeting by name, "Happening in the team", four ready-made ways to
start, and the team's rooms. Name search sits in the header.

## Before you start

- You are signed in. Every account has a personal organisation with the **Personal** badge;
  it is fine for working alone or with an interest group.
- From a room, click the logo or choose **All rooms** in the account menu.

## Steps

1. Open the home. Result: the address is `?org=…`, the room is closed, and the greeting
   names you.
2. Choose an organisation in the **Organisation** field. Result: the list shows the new
   organisation's rooms and the previous room is left behind.
3. Start from ready-made: **Story map**, **Roadmap**, **Roadmap example** or **Kanban room**.
   Result: the new-room dialog opens with that template chosen.
4. Click **New room** to create an empty room. Result: the room opens.
5. Choose a room in **Team rooms**. Result: the room opens; the tile names who created it and
   when it last changed.
6. Search by name in the header. Result: the list narrows; an empty answer says nothing
   matches.
7. When there is more than one page of rooms, sorting (**Updated**, **Created**, **Name**,
   **Author**), the direction arrow and 24-tile pages appear. On any tile open **⋯ → Cover and icon**. Type an emoji name or paste the emoji into the search box. Your choice appears beside the title in the directory and in the room.
8. **Trash** beside the rooms heading shows deleted rooms; **Restore** puts a room back.

The cover shows the canvas. To feature a frame, open its context menu and choose **Make cover**. Choose **Use whole room as cover** in the same menu to restore the canvas view. After an edit, the preview refreshes automatically at most once every ten minutes, even when the room is closed.

## Check

- The greeting names you, not the organisation.
- The top update appears below your name. Up to two more appear under **Happening in the team**. When there are no updates, you see a tip under your name.
- From an update you can reply in a thread, review an agent proposal, join a teammate in a room, or invite a new teammate. The close button hides the update in this browser.
- A tile carries the title, author and when the room changed; the emoji survives a reload.
- A deleted room appears in the trash and restores back to the list.
- Search finds a room by part of its name.

## Limits

- The author is the creator; handing over ownership does not rewrite history.
- Search looks at the room name, not the text on the canvas.
